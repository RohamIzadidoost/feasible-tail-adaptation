\begin{abstract}
Confident-tail test-time adaptation (TTA) pseudo-labels the extreme quantiles of
a detector's scores on unlabelled target audio and self-trains on them. The rule
is \emph{infeasible} wherever the deployment pool is class-skewed: a counting
bound caps pseudo-label purity at $\min(1,\pi_c/q_c)$ for any detector, so a
symmetric budget $q$ needs $q\le\min(\pi,1{-}\pi)$. We propose
\textbf{Feasible-Tail Adaptation (FTA)}, which sizes each tail to the class it is
meant to contain, $q_c{=}2q\hat\pi_c$ from a label-free prior estimate, capped by
a prevalence bound where no estimate can be trusted. FTA is identical to the
symmetric rule on balanced pools and repairs it where it fails. On
ASVspoof2021-DF ($97.2\%$ spoof) it lifts pseudo-real tail purity from $0.09$ to
$0.99$, turns a $63.3\%$ accuracy collapse back into $98.9\%$, and improves EER
on $8$ of $9$ runs ($4.30\!\to\!3.81$); on In-the-Wild resampled to $90$--$97\%$
fake it adds $\sim\!9$ points of balanced accuracy.
\end{abstract}

\begin{keywords}
audio deepfake detection, anti-spoofing, test-time adaptation, label shift,
cross-corpus generalization.
\end{keywords}

\section{Introduction}
Self-supervised (SSL) front-ends such as wav2vec\,2.0 and XLS-R, paired with
lightweight back-ends, reach very low in-domain error on standard anti-spoofing
benchmarks~\cite{wav2vec2aug,xlsrsls,aasist}. Deployed on audio from a
different source, accuracy drops steeply, a well-documented generalization gap~\cite{asdg,crossdomain,itw}.

We start from a diagnostic measurement: a fine-tuned XLS-R detector on an unseen
corpus keeps a high \textbf{ROC-AUC while its accuracy at a fixed threshold
collapses}. It still \emph{ranks} clips correctly, so its most-confident
predictions on unlabelled target audio are trustworthy enough to adapt on.

Every confident-tail method takes the same step---label the extreme quantiles of
the target scores and self-train on them---and that step carries an assumption
that is invisible on the benchmarks the field uses and fatal on the pools it
deploys to: that the target pool is roughly class-balanced. Take the top and
bottom $30\%$ of a $97\%$-spoof pool and the ``confidently bona fide'' bucket is
mechanically made of low-scoring \emph{spoof}. This is not hypothetical: it is
what happens to all three publicly released checkpoints we test on the official
ASVspoof2021-DF protocol (Table~\ref{tab:protoa}), and to three of four modern
TTA baselines.

\textbf{Feasible-Tail Adaptation.} The fix follows from asking how large a tail
may be: a bucket of $q_cN$ clips cannot hold more than the $\pi_cN$ clips of
class $c$ that exist, so no detector can push its purity past
$\min(1,\pi_c/q_c)$. FTA sizes each tail to its class, $q_c{=}2q\hat\pi_c$, from a
label-free prior estimate, and where no estimate can be trusted caps $q$ by a
prevalence lower bound instead. At $\hat\pi{=}\tfrac12$ it is \emph{identical} to
the symmetric rule: it generalizes the recipe it repairs, and inherits its
behaviour on the balanced pools where that recipe was validated.

\textbf{Contributions.} (i) FTA: a prevalence-aware tail budget that is a strict
generalization of the symmetric rule, needs no target label, and costs one scalar
per class. (ii) The feasibility constraint it is built from, $q_c\le\pi_c$ ---
exact, detector-independent, and confirmed by intervening on prevalence directly.
(iii) An evaluation on released checkpoints at the benchmark's own prevalence:
damage prevention on DF, balanced-accuracy gains on skewed In-the-Wild pools,
parity on balanced pools, operating-point repair over $369$ third-party cells.
(iv) The scope condition: the prior estimate degrades as the source's operating
point does ($r{=}0.997$), so FTA ships with an estimator-free cap and two
label-free monitors, one recovering $13.9$ EER points from a Tent collapse.

\textbf{Related work.} SSL front-ends with AASIST or attentive back-ends dominate
recent benchmarks~\cite{wav2vec2aug,xlsrsls,aasist,rawnet2,wav2dftsl}. Train-time domain
generalization aligns source domains~\cite{asdg} or runs gradient reversal over
source and target jointly~\cite{dann}; both act before deployment. In TTA,
entropy minimisation (Tent)~\cite{tent} is the reference recipe, and its
instability prompted reliable-sample filtering (ETA/EATA)~\cite{eata},
sharpness-aware entropy with recovery (SAR)~\cite{sar}, and information maximisation with
self-labelling (SHOT)~\cite{shot} --- we compare against all four. Closest to us is T$^2$A~\cite{t2a}, which also targets deepfake
TTA and diagnoses entropy minimisation's confirmation bias, replacing it with
uncertainty-aware negative learning on image/video deepfakes; it repairs the
\emph{objective}, while we show the \emph{sampling} carries a prevalence
precondition no objective can remove. Adaptation under label shift is itself established --- Label Shift
Adapter~\cite{lsa} adapts from an estimated target prior, RLSbench~\cite{rlsbench}
benchmarks relaxed label shift --- but both take the estimate as given, whereas
the bound below constrains any tail budget without one, and the estimate degrades
exactly where it is needed (Sec.~\ref{sec:ident}). The prior estimator is
black-box shift estimation~\cite{bbse}; the proxy $\mathcal{A}$-distance
is from~\cite{bendavid}.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
Let $x$ be a waveform with label $y\in\{0,1\}$ (fake$=1$) and let $f_\theta$
produce $s_\theta(x)=P_\theta(y{=}1\mid x)$. We are given a source model, its
labelled training corpus $\mathcal{S}$, and an \emph{unlabelled} target pool
$\mathcal{T}$. No target label is read at any point.

\textbf{What breaks under corpus shift.} AUC is threshold-free; accuracy at a
fixed $\tau$ is not, and empirically AUC degrades far less: across $369$
third-party cells the accuracy a model's ranking supports but its shipped
threshold does not deliver averages $7.29$ points. The deployable object is the
score \emph{order}; the broken one is the operating point.

\textbf{(a) How large may a tail be?} The symmetric rule --- our own earlier
configuration, an instance of confidence-based pseudo-label
selection~\cite{fixmatch,shot} --- takes the bottom $q$ and top $q$ of the target
scores as pseudo-real and pseudo-fake. Its ceiling needs no model at all. A
bucket of $q_cN$ clips can contain at most the pool's $\pi_cN$ class-$c$ clips,
so \emph{for any detector whatsoever}
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{purity}_c\;\le\;\min\!\big(1,\ \pi_c/q_c\big).
\label{eq:purity}
\end{equation}
This is counting, not an approximation; what is empirical is its
\emph{tightness}. Where prevalence binds, it is tight: on DF the realised tails
sit within $0.0003$ of the bound (Fig.~\ref{fig:mech}a). Where it is slack, the
ranking decides how much of the headroom is used. A symmetric budget therefore admits pure
pseudo-labels only while
\begin{equation}
q\;\le\;\min(\pi,\,1-\pi),
\label{eq:valid}
\end{equation}
which In-the-Wild satisfies at $q{=}0.3$ ($\min{=}.372$) and DF does not
($\min{=}.028$): there no detector can exceed $9.3\%$ pseudo-real purity, we
measure $8.9\%$. The damage is one-sided --- DF's pseudo-\emph{fake} tail is pure
at every $q$ --- which is what makes it repairable by budgeting rather than by a
better model.

\textbf{(b) Feasible-Tail Adaptation.} FTA gives each class the budget its own
prevalence can fill,
\begin{equation}
q_c\;=\;2q\,\hat\pi_c,
\label{eq:fta}
\end{equation}
so Eq.~\eqref{eq:purity} is satisfied for \emph{any} $q\le\tfrac12$ under a
correct prior, and $q_c{=}q$ at $\hat\pi{=}\tfrac12$: on a balanced pool FTA
\emph{is} the symmetric rule, byte for byte. With an estimate it needs
\begin{equation}
\hat\pi_c/\pi_c\;\le\;1/(2q)\quad\text{for both classes,}
\label{eq:tol}
\end{equation}
i.e.\ at $q{=}0.3$ the estimate may overstate either class by at most
$1.67\times$ --- satisfied on DF ($1.03$) and violated on the one cell where a
source model arrives badly mis-calibrated (Sec.~\ref{sec:ident}). A budget needs
the prior only to within a factor, not exactly, which is why an estimator that is
unidentifiable in general is still usable here. Because $q$ is
ours to choose and $\pi$ is not, FTA also admits an \emph{estimator-free}
instantiation: cap $q$ at a lower bound on $\min(\pi,1{-}\pi)$, buying safety
with supervision ($q{=}0.02$ labels $800$ of $20{,}000$ clips against
$12{,}000$). We report both, and a fixed-confidence prior-free control.

\textbf{(c) The prior, label-free.} $\hat\pi$ comes from black-box shift
estimation~\cite{bbse}: $\hat p = M^{-\top}h$ with $M$ the source confusion
matrix and $h$ the target prediction histogram; a score mixture and an oracle
prior are controls~\cite{sld}.

\textbf{(d) Objective and guards.} With pseudo-labels $\tilde y$ from (b) on the
confident set $\mathcal{C}$ and a stochastic channel perturbation $a(\cdot)$
(per-clip gain $g\sim\mathcal U(0.7,1.3)$ plus Gaussian noise $\sigma{=}0.005$),
\begin{equation}
\min_{\{\gamma,\beta\},\phi}\
\underbrace{\textstyle\sum_{x\in\mathcal C}\!\mathrm{CE}\big(f_\theta(x),\tilde y\big)}_{\mathcal L_{\mathrm{ST}}}
+\ \lambda\,\underbrace{\big\|f_\theta(a(x))-\mathrm{sg}[f_\theta(x)]\big\|^2}_{\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cons}}},
\end{equation}
optimising only the LayerNorm affine parameters $\{\gamma,\beta\}$ of the
fine-tuned blocks and the head $\phi$ ($16{,}706$ parameters), $\lambda{=}0.3$.
$\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cons}}$ is inherited from the base recipe and is \emph{not}
part of the contribution: dropping it moves DF EER by $0.08$ ($3.98$ vs.\
$4.06$) and costs less balanced accuracy ($86.58$ vs.\ $82.34$ on seed~2). FTA
is the budget, with or without it. Two
label-free statistics guard the run at deployment --- rank agreement with the
frozen source and the drift of the predicted positive rate from $\hat\pi$
(Sec.~\ref{sec:guards}).

\section{Experimental Setup}
\textbf{Protocol A.} The official ASVspoof2021-DF eval partition~\cite{asvspoof21}, scored against
the official CM keys. Of its $533{,}928$ eval trials we hold $400{,}435$
($74.998\%$, with similar coverage of either class), so our numbers are pool
numbers and we make no full-set or SOTA comparison. True spoof rate $97.22\%$.
We adapt \emph{released} checkpoints: DeepFense
XLS-R\,300M\,+\,AASIST trained on ASVspoof2019-LA~\cite{deepfensehf}, three
independent training seeds. Adaptation uses an unlabelled $20{,}000$-clip subsample; we report EER over the
pool we hold, an inductive number over trials \emph{not} in it, and two further
seeds on a fixed $50{,}000$ subset: nine runs. Metrics come from a re-audit of the cached score
arrays (interpolated ROC crossing); Protocol B keeps its per-fold EERs.

\textbf{Protocol B.} Leave-one-corpus-out over ASVspoof2019-LA~\cite{asvspoof19},
D2 (LibriSpeech vs.\ eight open TTS systems), In-the-Wild~\cite{itw} and the
Arabic set~\cite{arad}; each source pool merges the other three with a
38-language MLAAD subset~\cite{mlaad}; target audio never appears in any source
pool, and six MLAAD languages are held out of every pool. All methods run
on the same twelve folds. \textbf{Protocol C} is all $31{,}779$ In-the-Wild clips
at their native $37.2\%$ fake rate, skewed the \emph{opposite} way to DF.

\textbf{Comparison points.} Source-only; the median-threshold rule; Tent, IM-PL,
ETA/EATA and SAR; AdaBN~\cite{adabn}; DANN~\cite{dann}, which sees the held-out
target's unlabeled audio at every step; an ASDG-style baseline~\cite{asdg}; and
a target-supervised oracle. Every TTA arm adapts the same $16{,}706$ parameters
with the same lr, batch and pass count; none was tuned separately, since
per-target tuning needs the labels the setting assumes absent. EATA's redundancy
filter and SAR's recovery trigger are degenerate at $C{=}2$ and were re-derived
at their relative positions ($e_0{=}(0.2/\ln 1000)\ln C$).

\textbf{Implementation.} 16\,kHz audio, top-4-layer XLS-R fine-tuning for our own
source models (Adam, lr\,$2{\times}10^{-4}$, batch 32, 8 epochs). Adaptation:
lr\,$1{\times}10^{-4}$, $E{=}4$ passes, batch $32$ (third-party crops matched to
each checkpoint's own training length). Code and manifests:
\texttt{github.com/}\-\texttt{RohamIzadidoost/}\-\texttt{feasible-tail-adaptation},
with per-arm score dumps that reproduce every number here without a GPU.

\subsection{FTA on the official DF protocol}
Table~\ref{tab:protoa} is the main result. At this pool's prevalence the symmetric budget cannot work and does not:
accuracy $98.92\!\to\!63.33\%$, EER $4.30\!\to\!4.87$, a $35$-point threshold
deficit where there was none. FTA, changing one scalar per class, keeps the
accuracy ($98.89\%$) and improves the ranking ($4.30\!\to\!3.72$). The mechanism is the one Eq.~\eqref{eq:purity} predicts:
BBSE returns $\hat\pi{=}0.972$ against a true $0.9722$, the pseudo-real budget
shrinks $0.30\!\to\!0.014$, and that tail's purity rises $0.089\!\to\!0.993$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:mech}a).

Across the nine runs (three checkpoints $\times$ three adaptation runs; reused
checkpoints and overlapping pools make these sensitivity checks, not independent
trials) FTA beats the symmetric budget $9/9$ and the source $8/9$
($4.30\!\to\!3.81$ mean EER), $7/9$ on disjoint trials ($4.31\!\to\!3.83$). Accuracy separates the arms with no
overlap (Fig.~\ref{fig:mech}d). Two caveats: balanced accuracy falls on two
checkpoints and rises on the third ($78.35\!\to\!82.55$), a loss that tracks
$\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cons}}$ rather than the budget; and DF-240 gains nothing on
disjoint trials, so the improvement is carried by the two checkpoints with the
larger deficits.

The baselines fail through their own versions of the balance assumption: IM-PL's
diversity term is maximised at a \emph{uniform} mean prediction (at $C{=}2$, a
hard prior of $\tfrac12$), and ETA's filter admitted $99.8\%$ of the high-entropy
samples it exists to discard, reducing it to Tent --- an entropy key separates
confident from uncertain, not confident-and-right from confident-and-wrong. SAR
alone avoids damage, by not moving the model.

\subsection{At native prevalence, FTA inherits the recipe}
In-the-Wild at its native $0.372$ satisfies Eq.~\eqref{eq:valid} at $q{=}0.3$,
where FTA and the symmetric rule nearly coincide and both repair rather than
destroy. DeepFense s42 arrives ranking at AUC $0.917$ while correct on $39.1\%$
of clips, fewer than a majority-class predictor; adaptation drives the three
mis-calibrated checkpoints' deficits from $22.9$, $16.8$ and $48.3$ to $3.3$,
$0.1$ and $8.3$, and raises s42's \textbf{AUC to $.947$} --- which no threshold
move can do --- with the disjoint half slightly better, so the ranking gain is
not memorisation.

\subsection{Validating the budget rule by intervention}
Sweeping $q$ on DF s2 with everything else fixed drives accuracy
$98.96\!\to\!62.96$ as the bound falls $1.000\!\to\!0.093$ (Spearman $+1.000$;
Fig.~\ref{fig:mech}b). At $q{=}0.02$, FTA's estimator-free instantiation here,
adaptation is safe and improves on source ($4.51\!\to\!4.19$) with no prior at
all, but stays weaker than the estimated budget ($4.06$): shrinking $q$ discards
the majority tail too.

\textbf{Inducing the failure where the recipe works.} Resampling In-the-Wild ---
where $q{=}0.3$ is feasible --- to $P(\mathrm{fake})\in\{0.90,0.95,0.97\}$ with
audio and weights fixed makes accuracy track the bound (Spearman $+0.966$;
Fig.~\ref{fig:mech}c): the symmetric $q{=}0.3$ collapses ($73.3/68.5/65.4\%$),
$q{=}0.1$ degrades only where its own bound does ($95.9/92.6/89.4$) and
$q{=}0.02$ is unharmed ($97.8/98.6/98.6$), while FTA holds $97.7/98.7/98.9\%$ and
gains $\sim\!9$ points of \emph{balanced} accuracy on the source
($93.7/91.6/92.3$ against $82.7/83.6/83.5$): the failure is prevalence, not DF.

\subsection{Balanced pools: FTA is the recipe it generalizes}
Where Eq.~\eqref{eq:valid} holds, $\hat\pi\!\approx\!\tfrac12$ and FTA reduces to
the symmetric rule, so this arm confirms the generalization costs nothing
(Table~\ref{tab:protob}). FTA improves $10/12$ folds over source and beats every entropy-based arm, while
IM-PL ties it ($17.76$ vs.\ $17.44$, $p{=}0.47$): a hard uniform prior is free
where Eq.~\eqref{eq:valid} holds and destructive where it does not, so the two
separate only in the untested regime. Train-time baselines adapt less (DANN
$20.72$, ASDG-style $20.42$); AdaBN is the source itself ($18.57$).

\subsection{Label-free guards}
\label{sec:guards}
Checking Eq.~\eqref{eq:valid} needs $\pi$, not identifiable
(Sec.~\ref{sec:ident}), so a deployer asks the weaker question --- \emph{did}
this adaptation help? --- answered by two statistics: rank agreement with the
frozen source $\rho=\mathrm{Spearman}(s_\theta,s_{\theta_0})$ and the drift of the
predicted positive rate from $\hat\pi$. $\rho$ catches ranking collapse (ETA
$0.31$, Tent $0.44$), drift catches operating-point collapse ($0.37$, $0.39$,
$0.23$), and together they separate the five harmful arms from the three harmless
ones for any $\tau\in[0.70,0.80]$; as a leave-one-target-out stopping rule $\rho$
recovers $13.9$ EER points from Tent's collapse (oracle $17.24$), never firing on
FTA.

\subsection{The target prior is not identifiable}
\label{sec:ident}
A label-free estimate cannot be trusted far, for structural reasons rather than
any defect of BBSE: it observes only $\Pr[\hat
y{=}1]=\pi\,\mathrm{TPR}_{\mathcal T}+(1{-}\pi)\mathrm{FPR}_{\mathcal T}$, one
equation in three unknowns, and closes it by assuming source error rates carry
over --- what covariate shift breaks. An extreme prior and an extreme
calibration failure are then \emph{indistinguishable} (DF/s2 predicts $97.4\%$
fake at a true $0.972$, ITW/s42 $98.1\%$ at a true $0.372$), and $|\hat\pi-\pi|$
tracks the source deficit at $r{=}0.997$ over ten cells: estimating the prior and
detecting that the estimate is wrong are one problem. ITW/s42 is accordingly the
one cell where the estimated budget trails its own symmetric special case
($14.54$ EER against $12.58$; $11.59$ with the true prior), which is what the
estimator-free instantiation is for --- Eq.~\eqref{eq:valid} needs only a
\emph{lower bound} on $\min(\pi,1{-}\pi)$, and $q{=}0.02$ was safe on every cell
here.

\subsection{Breadth, and the mechanism}
Over $369$ third-party cells --- nine released checkpoints (five architectures,
incl.\ AST~\cite{ast} and ShiftySpeech's SSL-AASIST~\cite{shiftyspeech}) on the
$76$ pairs with no training overlap --- the effect is \emph{re-calibration}:
$\delta$ falls $7.29\!\to\!1.96$ ($248/369$, $p{=}2.2{\times}10^{-17}$) while a
proxy $\mathcal{A}$-distance and a domain-origin probe both \emph{rise} (Arabic
$1.545\!\to\!1.606$): repair, not invariance. Eq.~\eqref{eq:valid} is necessary,
not sufficient.
